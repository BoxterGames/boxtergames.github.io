  • Device or advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and Firebase App Instance ID

Data Retention and Deletion Requests

How to request deletion: Email gboxtergames@gmail.com with the subject “Fractal Puzzle data deletion request”. State that you want data associated with Fractal Puzzle deleted. You may include the Android Advertising ID or Firebase App Instance ID if available; do not send passwords or other sensitive information. We will review the request and respond within 30 days.

We can delete information that we directly store or control. Advertising and analytics information is processed by Google; Google handles its retention and deletion under its own policies. For Firebase Analytics, the standard user-level data retention choices are 2 or 14 months, and expired data is deleted on a monthly schedule. Aggregated reports may remain. AdMob retention is governed by Google's service policies and is not controlled by a 30-day deletion setting from the Application developer. We therefore cannot promise that all information held by Google is automatically deleted within 30 days.

Information stored locally by the Application can be removed by clearing the Application's storage or uninstalling it. Google also provides controls to review and manage information associated with your Google account at Google Account privacy settings.
