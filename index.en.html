ANATOMY OF A CASE STUDY

First, spark interest.
Then reveal the details.

  1. First screen · five seconds

    What it is, who it is for and its main value, with one visual and three features.

  2. Context · a short paragraph

    The task and constraints, team contribution, platform and status.

  3. Decisions · show and explain

    Two or three key details, with visuals and a short explanation.

  4. Result · provide evidence

    The outcome, real metrics, a demo or a testimonial when available, and a clear next step.

  5. Next project · keep exploring

    The next project, arrows and a persistent project panel keep the catalog accessible.
